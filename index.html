Psychometrics with the IRW: course map

DRAFT
This course is under construction and is not ready for teaching. Most lessons are still outlines or stubs, the few drafted ones will change, and nothing here has been checked by anyone but its authors. Please don't cite, assign, or circulate it yet. Comments are very welcome: bdomingue@stanford.edu.

These are stand-alone lessons on psychometrics, each built around open data from the Item Response Warehouse. You can teach them in sequence as a full course or pick out individual lessons. The core lessons make a first course in psychometrics; lessons marked optional go further. Each lesson lists what it assumes and what builds on it. The lessons grew out of EDUC 252 (Introduction to Psychometrics) at Stanford.
